VGUARD 0.1.2-CANDIDATE — SECURITY HARDENING
23 October 2026

1. Fixed a reproduced source-level VPN revocation race: an onRevoke callback inside establishment could previously be overwritten by a newly published ACTIVE session. A lifecycle epoch and atomic session publication now reject/close that replacement.
2. Fixed a reproduced Android UID/shared-group race that could route an unselected sibling. Package events are tracked during initial startup; complete identities are checked before and after establishment.
3. Added FLAG_SECURE for ordinary screenshot/recents/casting privacy and explicit cloud/device-transfer exclusions.
4. Build requires the existing signing identity for the official candidate and checks its public certificate fingerprint. Signing key material stays outside source and releases.
